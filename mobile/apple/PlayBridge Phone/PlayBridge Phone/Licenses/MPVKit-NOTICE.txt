PlayBridge Phone uses the standard MPVKit 1.0.0 product.
MPVKit bundles libmpv, FFmpeg and supporting media libraries.
The standard MPVKit source and bundle are LGPL-3.0.
The full LGPL-3.0 text is MPVKit-LICENSE.txt.
LGPL-3.0 incorporates GPL-3.0. The full GPL-3.0 text is GPL-3.0.txt.
Supporting libraries keep their own copyrights and licences.
Exact upstream versions and the permissive licence texts are in
ThirdPartyComponents.txt.
The MPVKit-GPL product is not linked by the phone app.

The standard FFmpeg build passes --enable-version3 and does not pass
--enable-gpl. GMP and Nettle are used on the LGPL-3.0 side of their
dual licences.

Package.swift Library.version repeats build-repository tags for several
dependencies. Those tags are not the upstream versions. Use
ThirdPartyComponents.txt for the upstream version of each library.
