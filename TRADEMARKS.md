# PlayBridge name and logos

The MPL-2.0 licence grants rights to the code, not trademark rights (section 2.3).
The **PlayBridge** name and logos identify the official apps and project.

If you publish a modified fork, use a different product name and icon. Make clear
that it is your project; do not imply that PlayBridge maintains, endorses or
supports it. You may identify the original project in credits and licence notices.

You may redistribute unmodified official builds under their applicable licences,
keeping the existing name, icons and notices and identifying them accurately.
Descriptive or nominative references such as “works with PlayBridge” are welcome
when they are truthful and do not suggest endorsement.

These rules do not claim that the name or logos are registered trademarks.
For permission to use the branding beyond these cases, contact the maintainer at
[playbridgeapp@gmail.com](mailto:playbridgeapp@gmail.com).
