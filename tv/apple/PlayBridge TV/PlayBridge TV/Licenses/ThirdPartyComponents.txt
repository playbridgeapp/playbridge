Third-party components
======================

This file is the same in the PlayBridge phone app and the PlayBridge TV app.
A line that says "Apple TV only" or "phone only" applies to that app.

PlayBridge's own code is MPL-2.0. That text is MPL-2.0.txt.
Source: https://github.com/playbridgeapp/playbridge

MPVKit
------

Product linked: MPVKit. The MPVKit-GPL product is not linked.
Package: https://github.com/mpvkit/MPVKit
Version: 1.0.0
Revision: 288527dffbc6d3e63cce147fc7b520c64a791603

The standard bundle is LGPL-3.0. The full text is MPVKit-LICENSE.txt.
That file is the LGPL-3.0 text of 29 June 2007, so a second LGPL-3.0.txt
is not included. LGPL-3.0 incorporates GPL-3.0. The full GPL-3.0 text is
GPL-3.0.txt.

The standard FFmpeg build passes --enable-version3 and does not pass
--enable-gpl. mpv is built with -Dgpl=false. GMP and Nettle are used on
the LGPL-3.0-or-later side of their dual licences, not the GPL-2.0 side.
The linked player is conveyed under LGPL-3.0, together with the permissive
notices below. LGPL-2.1-or-later pieces can be complied with under 2.1
for that component. The combination is not dropped to LGPL-2.1.

Package.swift Library.version repeats build-repository tags for several
dependencies. Those tags are not the upstream versions. The versions
below come from the dependency build scripts at the tags Package.swift
downloads.

Source and build scripts:
https://github.com/mpvkit/MPVKit/tree/1.0.0
https://github.com/mpvkit/MPVKit/blob/1.0.0/Package.swift
https://github.com/mpvkit/MPVKit/tree/1.0.0/Sources/BuildScripts

Not linked by the iOS or tvOS MPVKit product:
  libsmbclient 4.15.13-2512
  LuaJIT 2.1.0-fix
  the MPVKit-GPL binaries

libxml2, bz2, iconv, zlib, and libc++ are Apple SDK libraries named by
the MPVKit FFmpeg target. They are not vendored MPVKit binaries.
